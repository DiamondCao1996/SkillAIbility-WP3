Step 1 · Success metrics – assess your experience in the use case experiment

You tried a new technology in a SkillAIbility use case experiment. Tell us how it worked for you. First describe the task you did with it, then rate each success metric from your own experience and ★ star the ones that mattered most to you. Finish with what worked well and what should change. There are no right or wrong answers – your experience is the data. About 10–15 minutes.
