1 · Workforce canvasOpen-ended discussion and brainstorming around your own case. 2 · Assessment matrixOrganise the ideas into actionable requirement checklists, then design a linked solution. 3 · Use case matchingMatch your institute's use cases against the requirement lists and mark the gaps.

Step 1 · Organisation → Inclusion

For each worker group (rows) and each outcome the technology should deliver (columns), select which requirements apply. Cells are pre-filled with the WP3 research baseline – confirm, remove or add codes based on your own case. Click a cell to edit it; define new codes in the legend on the right (★ marks a frequent requirement). Your choices here feed the solution canvas in step 2.

Step 2 · Solution canvas

Design the solution for your case. The Persona box links to the worker groups of step 1, and the three Solution layers (task · technology · organisation) draw on the requirement codes you mapped in the matrix – codes ringed in yellow are the ones step 1 assigned to your selected personas. Click codes to include them, and add free text for anything beyond the codes.
