Step 3 · Assessment matrix – formalise requirements per worker group × learning pathway

Three dimensions, each a grid of worker groups (rows) × learning pathways (columns): Task characteristics (TA), Technology requirements (TE), Organisational conditions (OR). Cells are pre-filled with the WP3 research baseline – confirm, remove or add. Click a cell, then tick / untick codes in the legend on the right and add a note for the cell. Rename codes, add new ones (+ New code) or mark frequent ones with ★. ▼ Expand codes shows full text inside the cells; ▼ Expand list shows the full legend. The lists you build here flow into the solution canvas (step 4) and back to the use case toolkit.

Step 4 · Solution canvas

Design the solution, linked to the requirements you mapped. Your texts from the workforce canvas are already here – this canvas mirrors it. Click the three solution layers – Task characteristics (TA), Technology requirements (TE), Organisational conditions (OR) – and select the codes your solution builds on; codes assigned to your personas in the matrix are ringed in yellow as suggestions, and free text covers anything beyond the codes. Then reflect on the free-text boxes – challenge, pain points, goals, UVP, risks, KPIs, skills, action plan: does the solution still answer them? Refine where needed. When the group agrees: Submit all steps.
