Welcome

This toolkit runs a workshop entirely in your browser — no login, no install. Everything you type autosaves on this device, and one Submit sends the result to the research team as an Excel file.

Start by choosing the pathway that fits your group. Each pathway is a short, ordered sequence of tools. Every step opens with brief instructions and an estimated time before you enter it, so you always know what to do and how long it takes.

Tip: keep the same device/browser for the whole session — the tools share your data as you move between them.
